  • Demand letters

  • Settlement negotiations
  • Settlement conference preparation
  • Motions and procedural assistance
  • Trial preparation and representation where appropriate
  • Judgment enforcement guidance

Landlord & Tenant

Landlord & Tenant Board Services

Residential tenancy problems can become complicated quickly. Asiri Legal Services assists landlords and tenants with understanding their rights, preparing their case, and navigating proceedings before the Ontario Landlord and Tenant Board.

LANDLORDS

Landlord Legal Services

  • Non-payment of rent and rent arrears
  • Persistent late payment of rent
  • Eviction notices and applications
  • Damage to the rental unit or property
  • Substantial interference and tenant conduct issues
  • Landlord's own-use and purchaser-use matters
  • Renovation, demolition and conversion matters
  • Agreements to terminate a tenancy
  • Rent increases and related applications
  • Preparation and review of notices and applications
  • Evidence and hearing preparation
  • Settlement and payment agreements
  • Representation at LTB hearings
TENANTS

Tenant Legal Services

  • Eviction notices and applications
  • Rent arrears disputes
  • Rent increases and disputed charges
  • Maintenance and repair problems
  • Loss of vital services
  • Landlord interference or harassment
  • Illegal entry and privacy concerns
  • Substantial interference with reasonable enjoyment
  • Personal-use and purchaser-use eviction matters
  • Renovation and demolition eviction matters
  • Bad-faith eviction claims
  • Illegal charges and money collected by a landlord
  • Evidence and hearing preparation
  • Representation at LTB hearings

Notices, Applications & Hearing Preparation

  • Review and preparation of LTB notices and applications
  • Rent arrears calculations and payment records
  • Evidence and document organization
  • Photographs, communications and supporting records
  • Witness and hearing preparation
  • Settlement and mediation preparation
  • Procedural guidance before and after a hearing

The appropriate notice, application, remedy, and procedure depend on the individual circumstances of each matter. Representation is accepted only where permitted and after a conflict and scope review.

About

Meet Your Paralegal.

Abdulrahman Asiri, LL.M., Licensed Paralegal

Asiri Legal Services was founded by Abdulrahman Asiri, a Licensed Paralegal with the Law Society of Ontario since 2024.

Abdulrahman's legal education spans three jurisdictions. He earned his Bachelor of Laws in Saudi Arabia, completed his Master of Laws (LL.M.) in the United States, and later earned his Paralegal Diploma in Toronto, Ontario before becoming licensed to provide paralegal legal services in Ontario.

His professional experience includes substantial work involving Landlord and Tenant Board proceedings, legal research, document preparation, dispute resolution, negotiation, and administrative tribunal matters.

His approach is straightforward: understand the problem, explain the available options clearly, prepare carefully, and pursue a practical resolution.

Asiri Legal Services provides assistance to individuals, landlords, tenants, and small businesses with Landlord and Tenant Board matters, Small Claims Court matters, notary and commissioning services, and other matters within the permitted scope of Ontario paralegal practice.
